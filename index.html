About Our Foundation

Our foundation was established to spearhead impactful projects, addressing not only our direct community but the entire country. We work with various groups and communities, focusing on uplifting lives and creating sustainable solutions.

We currently have projects across multiple regions such as Batangas, Bohol, Misamis Oriental, Cagayan de Oro, and more. Through our efforts, we’ve touched over 15 million lives in the past 40 years, and we continue to extend our programs to more regions in need.
